Local adaptations: orthographic spherical projection, illustrative directional lighting, resizing to 640 pixels and transparent background. Generated by scripts/render_home_globes.ps1 from the original maps. Local filenames: earth.png, mars.png, moon.png. Changes do not imply endorsement by the original creator.

These are decorative globe illustrations, not raw scientific measurements or single-frame spacecraft photographs. Source maps use adjusted colors and may fill unmapped areas with fictional terrain. Bodies, lighting, sizes and spacing are not presented to scale or as current observations.

No AI-generated imagery, NASA logos or NASA endorsement claims are used. This work includes AI-assisted application code and a programmatic image projection script; disclose that assistance in the final project submission. Original NASA-derived texture references do not constitute completion of the project's measured scientific data requirements.
